Gallery

Screen recordings (AVI), RX audio recordings, screenshots and SSTV pictures stored on the radio. Screen recordings can be converted to MP4 in this browser or on the radio.

In-browser conversion needs a secure page. Open the HTTPS version of this page: the radio uses a self-signed certificate, so the browser shows a warning once. Accept it (Advanced, then Proceed) and the option appears. Converting on the radio works on both.
